Why GlowChem?

Most people buy skincare by hype. Ingredient lists are written in chemical names, and what irritates one face is harmless on another. A sensitive, redness-prone face and an oily, acne-prone face should read the same label very differently.

Step 1 – Scan. The YouCam AI Skin Analysis API scores redness, moisture, oiliness, acne, pores, texture, radiance and wrinkles from one selfie.

Step 2 – Irritant Watch. Your scan automatically sets your skin profile. Each ingredient in a product is checked against a chemistry-based irritant and comedogen database, weighted by how early it appears in the list (a rough guide to concentration) and by how relevant it is to your skin.

For shoppers and retailers: fewer returns and bad reactions, and a clear, explained reason behind every "good for you / not for you" recommendation.

GlowChem gives cosmetic guidance, not medical advice. If you have a skin condition, a reaction, or a known allergy, talk to a dermatologist or pharmacist. Ingredient flags are general: concentration and formulation matter, and patch-testing a new product is always sensible.
